It's been 7 weeks of doing a VO2 block where I've done one short interval training session and a high intensity interval training session a week. We'll see what the results are after I redo the step test.

This week though, I've been taking it easier since I went into the week feeling very exhausted. That got me thinking that maybe this was too much for right now. So I came up with the plan for the next block. There's 22 weeks until Nationals so we'll do 12 weeks of endurance and strength training before we spend the last 10 weeks doing specific prep.
## Endurance and Strength block
The priority for the block will be adaptation. After last year I wanted to test myself more to see if I was improving, which is one part of answering am I adapting. The second part is am I recovering. To know if I am recovering I'm going to rate the following things each morning:
- sleep
- soreness
- stress
- fatigue
- desire to train
- food
- yesterday's training performance

These should give me an indication of whether or not I am recovering. I found that by the end of the last block my desire to train had tanked. Training, even easy training, felt like a chore. The goal with tracking these things is to catch if I'm starting to dig a hole and stop digging it before I get too deep.

I'm going to switch up the basic week too:

| Day  | Focus    | Total duration of endurance training (min)  |
| ---- | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Sun  | Green    | 90+                                         |
| Mon  | Easy     | 45                                          |
| Tue  | Easy     | 45                                          |
| Wed  | Strength | 90 (45-60 min strength with the rest green) |
| Thur | Green    | 90                                          |
| Fri  | Easy     | 45                                          |
| Sat  | Green    | 150                                         |

This will give about 9.5 hrs of endurance training. Additionally, I want to still include light lifting to hit my weak spots like my hamstrings and rhomboids to balance out the sport specific muscles.

For loading days I'm going to include a standard warm up so that I can tell if I'm ready to load or not.
